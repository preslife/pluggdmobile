# Build 5 Physical-Device Recording

Record one continuous, legible walkthrough from a fresh Build 5 installation on
the registered iPhone 15 Pro Max running iOS 26.5.2 (23F84). Do not record a
password, private email, notification preview, payment detail or recovery code.

## Before recording

- [x] Confirm the exact model and iOS version in Settings → General → About: iPhone 15 Pro Max, iOS 26.5.2 (23F84).
- [ ] Verify the fan, creator and disposable deletion accounts immediately before recording.
- [ ] Put the device in Do Not Disturb and remove private notifications.
- [ ] Confirm the StoreKit sandbox account and wallet starting balance.
- [ ] Confirm published review-safe audio, one active Live room and the creator membership tier.
- [ ] Start from a deleted app / fresh install and portrait orientation.

## Required sequence

- [ ] Cold launch; open registration, then sign in with the review fan account.
- [ ] Play authorized audio on Home and move between tabs with uninterrupted mini-player playback.
- [ ] Open Discover destinations, the Carnival Hub, interactive map, route builder and event.
- [ ] Open a Community post, create a post, then demonstrate Report and Block.
- [ ] Open Wallet, show all five localized credit packs and complete one sandbox purchase.
- [ ] Enter a Live room, open the fixed-price gift tray, send a gift and show Report/Block controls.
- [ ] Open a creator membership; show title, duration, price, renewal wording, Terms, Privacy, subscription purchase and Restore Purchases.
- [ ] Demonstrate contextual camera, microphone, photos, contacts and notifications prompts only where the feature requests them.
- [ ] Sign in as the creator and open Creator Studio plus Live creation.
- [ ] Sign in with a disposable account and complete in-app account deletion.

## Evidence output
