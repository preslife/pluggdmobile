# App Store Screenshot Plan

Target device: iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator, portrait. Capture native-resolution images, then export the currently accepted 6.9-inch App Store Connect size without stretching. Do not use the existing 368×800 QA captures as store assets.

## Required sequence

1. **Music starts here** — Home with the first playable release and at least four visible choices.
2. **Explore every scene** — Discover showing scenes, genres, cities and a visible playable item.
3. **Hear the culture move** — Mixes or soundboards with distinctive artwork and playback controls.
4. **Find the room before it fills** — Events discovery with strong event artwork and clear location/date context.
5. **Follow artists, not noise** — Community with real cleared content; no fabricated live status or engagement.
6. **Your music keeps moving** — Full player with queue and playback context.
7. **Support what you discover** — Creator membership or credit surface with approved StoreKit products.
8. **You stay in control** — Privacy & safety showing report/block, content filters and account ownership.

## Capture rules

- Use a clean production-configured build and a dedicated screenshot account.
- Use cleared artwork and audio only; record each asset in `CONTENT_RIGHTS_REGISTER.md`.
- Avoid empty, loading, debug, invite-code and permission-dialog states.
- Keep status-bar time and device framing consistent.
- Never imply an artist is live, trending or endorsing PLUGGD unless that statement is true.
- Validate text legibility at App Store thumbnail size.
