2. PURPOSE AND ACCESS
PLUGGD is a music platform for fans and independent creators. Fans discover creator-authorized releases, albums/EPs, mixes and beats; explore editorial, scenes and events; participate in Community and Live; and support creators. Creators manage catalogue, community, video and Live activity through Creator Studio. Use the secure Sign-In Information fields; no invite or sample file is required. Registration is open.

3. REVIEW NAVIGATION
Home and Discover provide playback and editorial discovery. Discover opens Releases, Mixes/Listening Rooms, BeatPlug and PLUGGD TV. Community post/profile menus provide Report and Block; an owned post menu provides Delete. Events provides Search and Map. Avatar provides Appearance, Go Live, Create, Studio, Public page, Connect Card, Analytics, Wallet/Earnings, Library, Memberships, Purchases and PLUGGD Plans. Delete account: Avatar > Settings > Privacy & safety > Delete account.

4. APPLE PURCHASES
Apple consumables provide 500, 1,050, 2,750, 5,750 or 12,000 credits. Credits do not expire and are used only for release unlocks and fixed-value tips/Live gifts. Creator memberships are Apple auto-renewable subscriptions supporting individual artists. PLUGGD Starter, Creator and Pro are a separate auto-renewable subscription group for platform tools; Free is the no-charge default. Each paid tier has monthly/yearly options and App Store-localized pricing. Paths: Avatar > Wallet/Credits > Buy; Release > Download/Unlock; Live > Gifts; Creator profile > Shop > membership; Avatar > PLUGGD Plans. Subscription surfaces include renewal wording, Restore Purchases, Terms and Privacy. Professional beat licences, real-world tickets and physical goods are not bought with credits.

5. RIGHTS AND SAFETY
The signed ROWSON GROUP LTD attachment covers the review media. Media is PLUGGD-owned or backed by retrievable creator/partner authorization. Non-playable third-party catalogue metadata cannot enter the native player. Uploaders warrant rights. PLUGGD provides pre-publication moderation, reporting, blocking, filtering, user-owned deletion and public takedown/contact routes. Guidelines: https://www.pluggd.fm/community-guidelines. Contact: support@pluggd.fm.

6. SERVICES AND REGIONS
Supabase provides authentication/data/storage/server functions; Apple and Google provide sign-in; StoreKit and App Store Server API provide purchases/entitlements; Agora provides Live; Mapbox provides event maps without device location; Expo/APNs provides notifications. Stripe-hosted checkout is limited to policy-eligible professional licences, verified real-world tickets and physical goods. Apple prices localize by storefront; other core functions operate consistently across regions.

Submitted as an iPhone app only; no iPad platform is submitted. Terms: https://www.pluggd.fm/terms. Privacy: https://www.pluggd.fm/privacy.
