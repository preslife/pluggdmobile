# PLUGGD Build 13 — App Review video recording guide

Use the installed physical-device candidate: **PLUGGD 1.0.0 (13)** on the
iPhone 15 Pro Max. Record portrait, with Do Not Disturb enabled. Never show a
password, payment details, recovery code or private notification.

The videos are **App Review attachments**, not App Store previews and not part
of the binary. Keep the original files until review is complete.

## Recording format

For every new clip:

1. Start iPhone Screen Recording.
2. Hold on the first relevant PLUGGD screen for two seconds.
3. Perform the taps below slowly enough for the reviewer to follow.
4. Hold on each success/feedback screen for two seconds.
5. Stop recording and use the exact filename shown below.

A short opening title card is helpful but optional. If used, show only the clip
number and title for two seconds. Touch labels are not required; clear, slow
taps and visible screen feedback are sufficient.

## Clip 01 — Core music discovery and playback

Filename: `PLUGGD_B13_01_Music_Discovery.mov`

1. Cold-launch PLUGGD and show the Home screen.
2. Play the featured release and show the compact player.
3. Move to Discover while playback continues.
4. Open **Releases**, open a real release and expand the player.
5. Show Play/Pause, seek, Share and the single Like/Save action.
6. Open an album or EP containing more than one track and show the complete
   **This Release** track list; select another track.
7. Show the release's creator/artist name is tappable and opens the profile.
8. Return to Discover and open **Mixes / Listening Floor**.
9. Open a mix and its Listening Room; show transport, seek, L/R meters,
   comments, Save/Share and Track ID without posting private information.
10. Close the Listening Room with its X.
11. Open **BeatPlug**, audition a beat, move the Listening Bench progress and
    open the licence/lease information. Do not complete external checkout.

## Clip 02 — Community, moderation and user controls

Filename: `PLUGGD_B13_02_Community_Safety.mov`

1. Open **Community** and briefly show Feed, Boards, Explore and Maps.
2. Open a normal post, then open its image/full-post view and show the readable
   caption plus **more/less** when available.
3. Open the menu on a post owned by the signed-in App Review account and show
   **Delete**. Delete only a disposable review post and hold on the success
   feedback.
4. Open a harmless comment/post belonging to another account.
5. Open its menu, choose **Report**, select a reason, submit, and hold on the
   success feedback.
6. Open that user's profile/menu, choose **Block**, confirm, and hold on the
   feedback. Unblock after recording if the same account is needed again.
7. Open Community Guidelines or the public Support/contact route.

### Preparing the moderation-demo comment

The app intentionally hides identities named `App Review`, `Test Account` or
similar from the public feed. It also prevents clearly unsafe text from being
published when moderation catches it. Do **not** change Build 13 for this.

Use `test2.support@pluggd.fm` with a normal public display name such as
**Community Safety Demo**, and post a harmless comment such as **Safety control
demonstration comment**. If it is still absent, ensure the App Review account
has not already blocked that account. The Report action can be demonstrated on
harmless content; do not publish genuine abuse merely for the recording.

## Clip 03 — Live viewer and creator controls

Filename: `PLUGGD_B13_03_Live.mov`

Record the viewer and host portions separately if two accounts/devices are
needed, then keep both files with clear `Viewer` and `Host` suffixes.

### Creator/host portion

1. Tap the top **Live** action, then **Go Live**.
2. Show Studio/Green Room preview, camera and microphone choices, permission
   state, camera flip/route controls and room type.
3. Show Community Room, Listening Party, Studio/Cook-up, Event-linked and
   Battle/Arena choices without fabricating an unavailable event.
4. Create one review-safe room and enter it as host.
5. Show the host remains the host after leaving and re-entering.
6. Show Manage, mute/moderation, end/delete-room controls and that self-only
   Follow/Report actions are not shown.
7. End/delete the disposable room and show confirmation.

### Viewer portion

1. Enter the review-safe room from Live as the second account.
2. Show creator identity, Follow, comments, tap-to-heart, Share and Gifts.
3. Open Gifts, show the credit price and return without an unnecessary second
   credit purchase.
4. Show Safety/Report/Block for another participant or host.

## Clip 04 — Apple purchases, fan membership and PLUGGD Plans

Filename: `PLUGGD_B13_04_Apple_Purchases.mov`

1. Open Avatar > **Wallet / Credits** > Buy Credits.
2. Scroll through all five Apple-localized credit packs.
3. Open one Apple purchase sheet and cancel it, or retain the already captured
   successful Sandbox purchase evidence. Do not expose Apple ID details.
4. Show **Restore Purchases** and its visible completion/feedback state.
5. Open a release and show that credits unlock/download music; listening stays
   available separately.
6. Open a creator profile > Shop > fan membership.
7. Show the membership title, period, Apple-localized price,
   renewal/cancellation wording, Restore Purchases, Terms and Privacy.
8. Open Avatar > **PLUGGD Plans**.
9. Show Free, Starter, Creator and Pro, including commission and benefits.
10. Switch the centered selector between **Monthly** and **Yearly** and show the
    localized prices update for each tier.
11. Tap one visible **Choose** action to open Apple's sheet, then cancel. If the
    Starter Sandbox purchase is already active, show the current-plan state.
12. Show PLUGGD Plans **Restore Purchases**, Terms and Privacy.
13. Briefly show Avatar menu access to PLUGGD Plans, Memberships and Purchases.

## Clip 05 — Creator Studio, profile and video

Filename: `PLUGGD_B13_05_Creator_Studio.mov`

1. Open Avatar > **Studio** and show Home, Apps, Create, Insights and More.
2. Open Create and show Post, Go Live, Upload Clip, Thread, Event and Creator
   Studio destinations.
3. Open Releases and show the complete release-upload fields, including primary
   and additional artists, metadata, artwork and audio—do not publish a new
   release solely for review.
4. Briefly open Beats, Mixes, Soundboards and Video upload/catalogue routes and
   show their real media fields.
5. Open the creator's public profile and show Music, Community, video when
   available, Shop/membership and Connect Card.
6. Open PLUGGD TV from Discover/Live and show published video opens correctly.

## Clip 06 — Events, Store, support and policies

Filename: `PLUGGD_B13_06_Events_Store_Support.mov`

1. Open Events and show Event Spotlight plus Upcoming Events.
2. Open Search, show compact When/City/Genre dropdowns, then open Map and a real
   event pin/detail. Return to Events.
3. Open one event, show ticket destination and return without buying.
4. Open Store, choose a physical product, select separate colour and size
   options, add it to basket, and show basket review without completing payment.
5. Open Avatar > Settings and show the clear Support/contact route.
6. Open Terms, Privacy and Community Guidelines and show each loads with a Back
   action.

## Existing account-lifecycle clip — reuse only

Filename: keep the existing account-creation/deletion recording with a clear
name, for example `PLUGGD_Account_Creation_Deletion.mov`.

Do not create or delete another account. Reuse the existing recording that
shows account creation, completed in-app deletion and the signed-out result.
Keep its original date/build context truthful; do not label the older clip as
Build 13 if a different build number is visible.

## Final check before uploading

For each clip:

- Watch the entire export and confirm taps, labels and feedback are readable.
- Confirm no password, Apple ID, notification or private data is visible.
- Confirm no internal words such as Agora, Supabase, API, stack, debug or
  environment appear in PLUGGD UI. Apple's own `[Environment: Sandbox]` message
  is acceptable in sandbox purchase evidence.
- Keep files in portrait `.mov` or `.mp4`, with descriptive filenames.
- Send the final filenames so the App Review Notes attachment list can be
  updated exactly before submission.
